# Changelog

All notable changes to the free Unhurried theme.

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)

- **Lorem Ipsum** in every content pattern (word for word, same lengths, so layouts are unchanged); body copy lengthened to two or three sentences. Header, footer, 404, search and comments keep real words. Dev blueprint seeds 12 Lorem posts in five described categories with ten tags, an author bio and a Topics page.
- **Marquee** (`unhurried/marquee`), first in the default header: an Ink line with the tagline and the three newest post titles (links), or nothing when there are neither. `unhurried_marquee_loop()` doubles the lines (the copy `aria-hidden`, its links `tabindex="-1"`) and CSS drifts the track by -50% over 70s; it pauses on hover and focus, runs the other way in RTL, and stands still under reduced motion. The sticky header wrapper's `top` subtracts the marquee's 34px (`--uh-marquee-h`), so the marquee scrolls away and the capsule stays at 10px.
- **Pages**: Work (`unhurried/page-work`), Pricing (`unhurried/page-pricing`) and Visit (`unhurried/page-visit`), plus a **Numbers** section (`unhurried/stats-row`). Menu dropdowns are styled as rounded cards.
- **Nested services**: `/services/{group}/{service}/` from ordinary child pages. `unhurried/child-pages` is a Query Loop flagged `unhurriedChildren`; `unhurried_child_pages_query()` (`query_loop_block_query_vars`) points it at the pages under the page being viewed, in menu order, and `unhurried_hide_empty_child_pages()` drops the section while there are none. The Services page pattern lists its groups this way. New page patterns: Service group (`unhurried/page-service-group`) and Service (`unhurried/page-service`), both built on `unhurried/service-head` (breadcrumbs, the page's own title and excerpt), so no page needs its title retyped. Pages support excerpts (the lede and the card text). The checklist gains an optional "Group your services" step: Add a service group (opens nested under Services) and Add a service. Demo: a flat menu (About, Work, Services, Pricing, Visit, Journal; Topics stays in the footer and the journal toolbar) with one dropdown, Services: Design, Photography, Consulting, two services each. In the editor the section previews recent pages; the live page shows its own. Footer menus (all three footers) are plain links: `showSubmenuIcon: false` and `.unhurried-nav-plain` hides submenus, so only the header has the dropdown.
- **Newsletter**: `unhurried_newsletter_form()` supplies a signup form from a plugin, since a theme may not collect addresses: the free, self-hosted Newsletter plugin's minimal form (a `[newsletter_form type="minimal"]` Shortcode block; `unhurried_render_newsletter_shortcode()` renders it inside template parts, which run shortcodes before their patterns, and names the email field), else Jetpack's Subscribe block, else block markup from the `unhurried_newsletter_block` filter; with none, a "Subscribe by RSS" button. Used by the footer row and the Newsletter band (`unhurried/signup-cta`, which lost its sample mailto button). Styled as the search pill. Newsletter joins the About screen's recommended plugins, and the Playground demo installs it.
- **Motion system.** Tokens in `theme.json` (`settings.custom.motion`: easeOut, easeInOut, durationPress 120ms, durationQuick 220ms, durationReveal 900ms, durationSlow 1400ms, stagger 70ms, distance 28px). `assets/js/reveal.js` (same handle) now marks every element in `main` and the footer with `data-uh` and a kind (rise, card, image, draw, count, words) and reveals it with an IntersectionObserver at 15% visibility; items entering together are staggered, capped at 8 steps; a marked element's descendants stay still; header, navigation, marquee, the spiral disc and an article's body paragraphs never move. `unhurried_motion_head()` prints a tiny inline script that adds `uh-motion` to `<html>` before first paint (main content is held back until the script marks it, with one forced style flush so the first screen animates), and removes it after 2.5s if the script never starts; filter `unhurried_motion`. Reduced motion, no script, the editor and print all show everything. The opt-in Reveal style still works and steps aside when the automatic mode is on. Feedback layer: buttons lift on hover and press on click (inside the 400ms Doherty window), link and menu underlines glide, dropdowns unfold, linked cards rise, post photos lean in, chips and fields answer, accordions open with `interpolate-size`, the gift card tilts toward the pointer with a glare, the map pin pings. Scroll-linked depth (`animation-timeline`, where supported): cover photos drift, the staggered gallery floats at two speeds, the hero sinks and its words recede, the header shadow deepens. Cross-document view transitions cross-fade pages while the header and marquee hold still. Opt out per block with `unhurried-still`.
- **Setup checklist** (`inc/setup.php`) on Appearance > About Unhurried, replacing the three-line Start here card: nine steps (name, homepage, posts page, pages, menu, social profiles, newsletter, forms, look) with live status, a progress bar and one button each. Create buttons open `post-new.php?post_type=page&unhurried_page=<slug>`; `unhurried_prefill_page()` (on `default_content`) fills the new auto-draft with the page pattern and sets its template, and `unhurried_prefill_title()` names it. Nothing is created until the user publishes, and a done step drops its Create button. `unhurried_site_editor_url()` builds Site Editor links for both route styles (6.6/6.7 and 6.8+). Filter: `unhurried_setup_steps`. A dismissible dashboard and Themes notice links to it until the steps are done.
- **Contact forms**: `unhurried_contact_form()` puts the oldest Contact Form 7 or WPForms form in the Contact card (`unhurried/contact-split`), else the Email us button; filter `unhurried_contact_form_block`. The checklist recommends Contact Form 7 on PHP 8.3+ (its current requirement) and WPForms Lite below. Plugins are detected by constant, not shortcode, so the Site Editor resolves the same markup as the front end (Newsletter registers its shortcode on the front end only; saving the footer in the editor used to swap the form for the RSS button). Plain form fields and submit buttons from any plugin take the theme's look at zero specificity (`:where()`), with Contact Form 7 label spacing.
- **Anchors**: `scroll-padding-top` clears the sticky header; smooth scrolling unless reduced motion.
- Playground demo: PHP 8.3, Contact Form 7 installed.
- **Link labels**: buttons and links that lead to a page now name it (Contact us, Our services, Read the journal →, Get directions, Email us) in every hero, the call to action, process panel, services accordion, gift card, pricing tiers, story rows, essay, map and contact patterns.
- **Footer**: a Newsletter row under the search (see above). Social icons (Google Business Profile, Instagram, Facebook, X, Pinterest, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Yelp) ship with "#" addresses; `unhurried_hide_placeholder_social()` hides those from visitors (people who can edit the site still see them, and the Playground demo shows all via `UNHURRIED_DEMO`), and an icon block left empty prints nothing.
- **Wordmark**: capitals (`text-transform: uppercase`), and the font size is now exactly `min(100cqi, 1360px) / --unhurried-wordmark-em`, with the em width measured in capitals, so any title fills the column. On hover (or keyboard focus) the letters roll up one after another and an accent-colored twin rises into place; `unhurried_wordmark_letters()` wraps the letters (`aria-hidden`) and repeats the title in a visually hidden span. Reduced motion: an accent color change instead.
- **Hero, statue** (`unhurried/hero-statue`) is the Homepage hero now: a Cover filling an inset, rounded frame at full screen height (less the header, plus the rising panel's overlap), the photo edge to edge, a shade that leans to the lower left under the headline, lede, buttons and a caption. The image is a 19th-century print of Michelangelo's David from the Rijksmuseum (CC0), head only. Phones get a bottom shade instead. Motion: the image unveils once; reduced motion: still.
- **Hero, spiral** (`unhurried/hero-spiral`), a second hero pattern: an inset Ink stage, a round image (`assets/images/spiral.webp`) turning once every 240s inside a hairline and a counter-turning dashed ring, the heading's italic word absolutely placed behind it (heading kept `position: static; z-index: auto`, since flex items honor z-index). `--uh-under` makes room for a following `.unhurried-rise` panel. Reduced motion: still.
- **Journal**: `core/breadcrumbs` on the posts page, archives, search and posts (`prefersTaxonomy` on posts); `unhurried/journal-toolbar` (All chip, `core/terms-query` category chips with counts, posts-only `core/search`, "All topics" link when /topics/ exists); cards gain the category chip and `core/post-time-to-read`; `core/query-total` above the grid; topic chips in the no-results state. `unhurried_mark_current_term()` (`render_block_core/term-name`) marks the current topic with `is-current` and `aria-current`.
- **Posts**: author name, date and reading time; tag chips; `core/post-author` box with bio; `unhurried/hidden-related-posts` (up to three from the same categories, nothing when none); a scroll-linked reading progress bar (`body.single-post::before`, `animation-timeline: scroll(root)`, behind `@supports`).
- **Topics directory** (`unhurried/page-topics`): category cards from `core/terms-query` (description, count via `unhurried_term_count_label()`, translated singular/plural) and tag chips.
- **Essay** (`unhurried/text-essay`): sticky aside, drop-cap lede, two text columns, pull quote, link to the journal. Accordion and gallery sections gain ledes; Services, accordion gains a hairline button.
- **Footer**: tagline plus a pill search, Explore, Topics (`core/categories` with counts) and Latest (`core/latest-posts`, three with dates), shown only when the site has posts; bottom row is the year and site name, Back to top (`#top`) and the credit.
- **Header**: the sticky element is now the template part wrapper (`header.wp-block-template-part:has(> .unhurried-header)`); the capsule was its only child and never stuck. `unhurried_mark_contact_link()` (`render_block_core/navigation`) tags the menu item that links to /contact/ so the capsule hides it beside the Contact button and the open mobile menu keeps it.
- These blocks need WordPress 6.9 or later to show (breadcrumbs, terms query, time to read, query total); on older versions they render nothing.
- Images: a warm still-life and light set (dried stems in vases, sand, leaf shadows, an abstract render for the hero disc), CC0 from StockSnap.io and WordPress.org Photos. Same filenames as 1.1.x, so pages built from older patterns pick up the new pictures.
- **WooCommerce, legibility only** (`inc/woocommerce.php`, new; the shop's layout stays Unhurried Pro's work). In a dark look (Nocturne, Moss, Harbor, Plum, Lagoon, Dusk, or a palette darkened by hand) the Checkout block's floating labels took the page's light text and sat on WooCommerce's white fields at about 1.1:1. `unhurried_is_dark_look()` compares the palette's `base` and `contrast`; when the page is the darker one the theme declares WordPress's own `dark-editor-style` support, which WooCommerce reads as the default of the Cart and Checkout blocks' "Dark mode inputs", so those blocks use WooCommerce's dark fields. `assets/css/woocommerce-dark.css` (dark looks) mixes WooCommerce's red and green halfway to the text color (`--wc-red`: required marks, field errors, the remove cross, 2:1 before; `--wc-green`: the edge of a checked field; both read from WooCommerce's own variables on the root through `--uh-wc-red` and `--uh-wc-green`, so the file holds no color of its own) and gives the white account fields an edge that shows against the page (`--wc-form-border-color`). `assets/css/woocommerce.css` (every look) raises the cart's quantity stepper to 3:1 (its edge was 30% of the text color) and its minus and plus to 4.5:1, and does the same for the older `[woocommerce_cart]` quantity and coupon fields. Both files are printed inline. None of this loads without WooCommerce or under Unhurried Pro; filters `unhurried_is_dark_look` and `unhurried_woocommerce_legibility`.
- **Translated sites**: the checklist found its pages by English slug while their titles, and so their addresses, are translated ("Контакт", /контакт/), so the pages step never completed, the header button never turned on and pattern buttons led to `#`. `unhurried_prefill_page()` now marks each page it opens with its key (post meta `_unhurried_page`), and `unhurried_find_page()` returns the page with the slug, as before, or else the marked one. `unhurried_page_link()`, `unhurried_page_exists()`, the header button and `unhurried_mark_contact_link()` all go through it. Sites with the English slugs behave as before. New filters `unhurried_page_id` (for multilingual plugins) and `unhurried_page_link`.
- **Motion, three fixes**: a target taller than the window enters once it fills 15% of the window (past about 6.7 screens it could never show 15% of itself, so a long form on a phone stayed hidden); anything marked `unhurried-still`, or inside it, is left out of the pre-paint hold, so those pages are readable at first paint with or without the script; count-up figures run in the figure's own format and end as exactly what the author wrote ("1,200" ended as "1200", "12,5" as "125", and markup was dropped).
- **Child themes**: `style.css` and `reveal.js` carry the parent theme's version (they took the child's, so a parent update left browsers on the old files). A child theme's own `assets/js/reveal.js` keeps the child's version.
- **About screen**: the Pro card and notice name what Pro adds (a header band, 4:5 product photos, a summary that follows the page, a checkout with nothing else on it) instead of "WooCommerce's default look"; no price in the three card strings; the 'proof' card says what stays and what Pro copies; both Pro links open in a new tab with `rel="noopener"`. The review notice names the real wait when `unhurried_notice_days` changes it.
- **Checklist**: "Set your menu" is done only for a menu someone made (`unhurried_has_menu()`; WordPress's automatic Page List menu does not count); plugin buttons are whole phrases for translators (`unhurried_plugin_action_label()`: "Install %s", "Activate %s", "View %s"); the title prefill applies to a new page only.
- **Automatic page list**: until a site has its own menu the Navigation block lists every published page. `unhurried_page_list_exclude()` keeps WooCommerce's Checkout page out of that list, and its Cart and My account pages too when the header part the page uses holds WooCommerce's cart and account icons (read as the site has it now, a header edited in the Site Editor included; without the icons, or with no header part, the two links stay); filter `unhurried_page_list_exclude`. This applies only to the automatic list, that is a Navigation block whose menu is nothing but one Page List block (`unhurried_navigation_is_automatic()`, `unhurried_is_page_list_only()`). A menu that holds anything else, a Page List block among other links included, is shown exactly as saved, and so is a Page List block placed outside a menu. The Site Editor's own preview of the automatic menu still lists every page.
- **Variation dropdowns** on WooCommerce product pages showed the top half of their text: the fields rule's `font: inherit` brought the table's 2em line height into a fixed-height select. `.woocommerce .variations_form table.variations select` gets `line-height: normal` and no vertical padding. The dropdown keeps its height; its row gets about 4.5 px shorter at 1440 (under 1 px on a phone), because the oversized line no longer pushes it down.
- `functions.php` and `inc/*.php` exit when requested directly (`ABSPATH`). Block attributes printed from PHP go through `serialize_block_attributes()` (search, social links, related posts), so a translation containing `--` cannot break a block comment.
- **Footer contrast**: category counts and post dates in the footer were at 60% of its text color and the search and newsletter placeholders at 55%; on the light footer of the dark looks that is 3.99 to 4.37:1. All are at 70% now (5.4:1 or more in every look).
- **Focus rings**: one variable, `--uh-focus`, used by `style.css` and by `theme.json`'s link and button focus. The accent on the page, as before; the section's text color on Ink sections, the marquee and contrast-colored groups (the accent is under 3:1 there in 17 of the 20 looks); fixed Bone on Glass; on Ground the accent leaned 20% toward the text color (plain accent is 2.7:1 on Terrace's Ground). On a Cover the ring is two-tone, white with a black band inside it (the reverse on a light Cover), because no single color shows on every photograph, and a Cover washed with the Contrast color is light in a dark look. Without Unhurried Pro, in a dark look, a link inside one of WooCommerce's notices takes the color of its own text for its ring (`assets/css/woocommerce-dark.css`; the accent was 1.4 to 1.8:1 on the notice). Search and comment fields, which removed the outline and showed a 1.1:1 glow, show the same 2px ring; the two pill searches draw it around the pill. Where a block hides its overflow the ring was cut off (the site title in the header and every post picture showed none, the footer wordmark half of one): with `:has()` the block now draws the ring itself.
- **Firefox and the sticky header**: controls get `scroll-margin-top: 96px` inside `@supports (-moz-appearance: none)`. Firefox ignores `scroll-padding-top` when Shift+Tab moves focus up and scrolled the control under the capsule. A control that is already partly on screen is not scrolled by Firefox at all, so a tall picture link can still start under the capsule; it is never wholly hidden.
- **Landmarks**: the header part and the two header patterns no longer wrap their content in a second `<header>` (a `<div>` with the same classes; a header saved in the Site Editor keeps its markup). `unhurried_label_navigation()` names the header and footer menus "Main" and "Footer" (they were unnamed and " 2"); `unhurried_label_search()` names each search form from the block's own words; the search shown when there are no posts is labeled "Search again". `unhurried_template_part_area()` and `unhurried_part_area()` tell which area is rendering, also for WooCommerce's templates, which do not always say. The motion script's list of things it never animates gains `.unhurried-header`, which the inner `<header>` used to cover.
- **Dark looks declare `color-scheme: dark`** (`assets/css/dark.css`, printed inline only in a dark look; Ink sections and the marquee, the light surfaces of a dark look, stay `light`). Scrollbars, native select lists, checkboxes, date pickers and autofill follow. Embedded frames (`iframe`, `embed`, `object`) keep the `light` scheme they had, so a transparent embed such as a card field is not given a white canvas. Light looks are unchanged. A field that a plugin half-styles (a fixed background with the browser's text color, or the reverse) can change with this: `wp_dequeue_style( 'unhurried-dark' )` switches it off.
- **Right to left**: the header capsule's uneven padding trades sides under `[dir="rtl"]`.
- **First paint (F-22).** The first screen no longer waits for `reveal.js`. Before, `html.uh-motion` held every heading, paragraph, picture and list item in `main` and the footer at opacity 0 until the deferred script ran (or the 2.5 s failsafe fired); a browser does not count an element at opacity 0 as painted, so on a slow phone the page's largest paint waited for the script. Now the first screen rises by CSS alone from the first paint: the outermost block of each kind runs `uh-arrive` (text), `uh-arrive-far` (pictures) or `uh-arrive-card` (cards, panels, details), from opacity .4 and 28 or 42 px below its place over `--uh-reveal` (900 ms), as DESIGN-GUIDE section 6 asks for the first screen. `reveal.js` then marks (`data-uh`) only what is below the window when it runs; what is on screen or above it finishes the CSS rise. The failsafe timer is gone: nothing is hidden before the script runs. With JavaScript off, the script blocked, reduced motion or in print everything shows, as before. Dividers and the wordmark still draw in from the script; a figure on the first screen rises with it instead of counting up. Lighthouse, phone, free alone, median of 5: home LCP 6013 to 4519 ms, About 5566 to 3026 ms, a post 3629 to 3339 ms; FCP unchanged.
- **Pattern pictures load as needed (F-22).** `unhurried_theme_image_size()` (`render_block_core/image`, `render_block_core/cover`) gives pictures from the theme's `assets/images/` the width and height of their file, so WordPress's loading rules apply to them (an image without a size is never lazy-loaded): the home page went from 1207 to 651 KiB. `unhurried_theme_image_loading()` (`wp_get_loading_optimization_attributes`) lets a bundled picture that comes after the page's priority image wait until it nears the window, where WordPress would load the first three at once (on About a 254 KB photograph at the foot of the page loaded with the hero). The first picture keeps `fetchpriority="high"`. Both run as the page is shown, so pages built from older patterns benefit too; Media Library images keep WordPress's own rules.
- **Text over photographs in the dark looks (F-24).** In Nocturne, Moss, Harbor, Plum, Lagoon and Dusk a Cover's shade (`has-contrast-background-color`) became a pale wash and its text (`has-base-color`) turned dark, because those two colors swap in a dark look: on the home page the Services Cover's button measured 1.4:1 and the Statement Cover's text 1.2 to 1.5:1. `assets/css/dark-covers.css` (printed inline, dark looks only, enqueued as `unhurried-dark-covers`) gives such a Cover the look's page color as its shade and the look's text color to its `has-base-color` text, so text over a photograph stays light on a dark shade, as in the light looks (DESIGN-GUIDE section 3, the Glass rule). A Cover set to a light shade (`is-light`) and blocks with a fill of their own inside a Cover keep their colors. Light looks load nothing new. It acts on the page as shown, so Covers already placed in pages are mended too.
- **Small text over photographs, every look (F-24 follow-up).** An eyebrow over a Cover is at full strength instead of 78% (`style.css`, `.wp-block-cover__inner-container .is-style-eyebrow`), and a Cover with the default 50% contrast shade that carries an eyebrow (the Services pattern and pages built from it; not the statue hero, not Covers with another shade) gets a 65% shade. The Services eyebrow was 2.7 to 3.3:1 in all 20 looks, the statue hero's eyebrow 4.3:1 at phone width, the Services lede 4.3:1 in the tinted papers at phone width; measured against the pixels behind the text, every text over the theme's Covers is now 5.0:1 or more (small text) and 5.6:1 or more (large text) in all 20 looks at 390 and 1440. A 60% shade left the tinted papers at 4.4:1. CSS only, so existing pages are mended; the pattern file is unchanged (its next shade step, 70%, would be darker than needed).
- **The heroes' own entrances start at .4 (F-36).** The accent word of the statue and spiral heroes (`unhurried-exhale`), the statue photograph (`unhurried-unveil`) and the spiral disc (`unhurried-emerge`) started from opacity 0: the word was missing for the first 0.6 s and became the home page's late largest paint, and the photograph's first frames showed bare paper. They start at opacity .4 now, like the rest of the first screen (DESIGN-GUIDE section 6). Timing, easing, filters, the word's 0.6 s delay and its letter-spacing are unchanged, and the word still rises with its heading (inside the heading's own rise from .4 it shows at about .16 at the first paint and .4 by 0.6 s). Lighthouse now counts the photograph from the first paint, so it is the home page's LCP element and the lab LCP waits for its download (147 KB): phone, free alone, median of 5, 4299 to 3948 ms; FCP and CLS unchanged.
- **Spiral hero: the large word is back at the foot of the stage (F-38).** Since the first screen rises by CSS (F-22), the spiral hero's heading kept its rise animation in effect (`uh-arrive`, fill mode `both`, so `translate` never returns to `none`), and an element with a translate in effect is the containing block of what is positioned inside it: from 782px the huge accent word was placed against the heading, over the title, instead of against the stage. From 782px that heading now enters by opacity alone (`uh-arrive-fade`, from .4, same duration and easing), so the word sits where it does on main; below 782px, where the word is in the line, the heading still rises. The other hero patterns (statue, split, staggered, type, type-contact) were checked at 390, 768 and 1440 with motion, reduced motion and JavaScript off: unaffected.
- **The home photograph arrives sooner on phones; lines no longer move when the fonts arrive (F-39).** The statue photograph keeps its file (`assets/images/david.webp`, 1800 × 2296, unchanged). Beside it are two smaller copies made from it, `david-1200.webp` (106 KiB) and `david-800.webp` (60 KiB): Lanczos resampling, a light unsharp mask and WebP quality 85, so the face keeps its grain and edges where they are shown. `unhurried_theme_image_srcset()` (`wp_content_img_tag`) gives a Cover's photograph from `assets/images/` a `srcset` of such copies and the original as the page is shown, so pages that already hold the pattern get it and nothing saved changes. Its `sizes` (`(max-width: 781px) 80vh, 1800px`) sends every screen 782px and wider to the original: desktops and tablets load exactly the file they loaded before, and so do larger 2x phones and every 3x phone; Lighthouse's phone, small 2x phones and 1x phones load a copy. It runs after WordPress has set the picture's loading and sizes, so WordPress does not add `auto` to these sizes on a lazy picture. The picture keeps `fetchpriority="high"` and is never lazy on the first screen. `style.css` adds three local fallback faces with the web fonts' metrics (`Hanken Grotesk Fallback` and `Inter Fallback` on Arial, `Fraunces Fallback` on Georgia Italic: `size-adjust` and ascent and descent overrides, nothing downloaded), and `theme.json` lists each right after its web font, so the swap keeps the line breaks: the home headline on a phone stays three lines (it went from five to three), and About's CLS went from 0.019 to 0.001. Lighthouse, phone, free alone, median of 5: home LCP 4283 to 3993 ms, 652 to 606 KiB requested before the LCP; on a throttled phone of Lighthouse's size the photograph paints at 2260 ms instead of 2800 ms. Preloading the display font or the photograph did not help (measured): the font preload delayed the first paint by about 110 ms.
- **Dark looks, follow-ups (F-26).** Native checkboxes and radio buttons keep the light scheme (`assets/css/dark.css`): Safari (WebKit) drew an unchecked one at 2.5 to 2.9:1 against a dark page; the white box is 18.4:1 in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox, as before 1.2.0. Boxes a plugin draws itself are not affected. New filter `unhurried_dark_color_scheme` (default true): return false to leave `dark.css` out, for a plugin field with only a white fill or only dark text; the Cover fix above stays. The two pill searches no longer show the soft field glow inside their focus ring. A menu holding nothing but one Page List counts as the automatic menu only while that Page List has no settings (`unhurried_is_page_list_only()`): WordPress saves exactly that as its own fallback, so the two cannot be told apart, while a Page List with a parent page chosen is a menu someone made (its pages are no longer filtered, and "Set your menu" is done). Not changed: WPForms Lite prints its label color as `--wpforms-label-color: rgba( 0, 0, 0, 0.85 )` on `:root` (`src/Frontend/CSSVars.php`), not an inherited color, so its labels stay near-black in a dark look, as in 1.1.1; that variable is the way to change it.
- The version header (`style.css`) and the Stable tag (`readme.txt`) read 1.2.0, so asset URLs and WordPress's per-version pattern cache change with this release. Not tagged.

## 1.1.2 (2026-11-19)

Nine fixes found by visiting the 13 live sites that use the theme (2026-11-19). Most sites never edit the header and footer, so whatever the theme prints there goes live as it stands. Plan: `docs/internal/2026-11-19-free-1.1.2-plan.md`.

- Footer with wordmark (`unhurried/footer`): the "Take your time." heading and the Visit column (sample address and `hello@example.com`) are gone. The site tagline is the lead line (`unhurried-footer-lead`: display face, x-large, 24ch, balanced); with no tagline only the Explore column shows. Footer, quiet loses its Visit column too.
- New pattern **Footer with visit details** (`unhurried/footer-visit`): the 1.1.1 layout, for sites that want an address column. Its email is a `mailto:` link, as is the one in Contact, split.
- Wordmark: `unhurried_size_wordmark()` (`render_block_core/site-title`) puts the title's estimated width in em on the element as `--unhurried-wordmark-em`, from glyph widths measured off the bundled Hanken Grotesk (`unhurried_wordmark_em_width()`); `style.css` caps the size at the column width divided by it. Titles up to about twelve characters keep the 15vw size, so "Unhurried" is unchanged. Reaches footers saved before 1.1.2 (they carry the class). `overflow-x: clip` replaces `overflow: hidden`, so descenders are no longer cut.
- Header button (`unhurried/header-button`): printed only when a published page with the slug `contact` exists, labeled with its title. Before, it read "Book a visit" and linked to `#` on sites without that page. Unhurried Pro's header uses the same pattern.
- Blog heading (`unhurried/hidden-blog-heading`): the posts page title with the site name as the eyebrow, else the tagline, else the site name alone; over 32 characters it steps down to xx-large. "Stories" and "Notes on craft" are removed.
