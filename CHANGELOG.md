# Changelog

All notable changes to the free Unhurried theme.

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)

- **Lorem Ipsum** in every content pattern (word for word, same lengths, so layouts are unchanged); body copy lengthened to two or three sentences. Header, footer, 404, search and comments keep real words. Dev blueprint seeds 12 Lorem posts in five described categories with ten tags, an author bio and a Topics page.
- **Marquee** (`unhurried/marquee`), first in the default header: an Ink line with the tagline and the three newest post titles (links), or nothing when there are neither. `unhurried_marquee_loop()` doubles the lines (the copy `aria-hidden`, its links `tabindex="-1"`) and CSS drifts the track by -50% over 70s; it pauses on hover and focus, runs the other way in RTL, and stands still under reduced motion. The sticky header wrapper's `top` subtracts the marquee's 34px (`--uh-marquee-h`), so the marquee scrolls away and the capsule stays at 10px.
- **Pages**: Work (`unhurried/page-work`), Pricing (`unhurried/page-pricing`) and Visit (`unhurried/page-visit`), plus a **Numbers** section (`unhurried/stats-row`). Menu dropdowns are styled as rounded cards.
- **Nested services**: `/services/{group}/{service}/` from ordinary child pages. `unhurried/child-pages` is a Query Loop flagged `unhurriedChildren`; `unhurried_child_pages_query()` (`query_loop_block_query_vars`) points it at the pages under the page being viewed, in menu order, and `unhurried_hide_empty_child_pages()` drops the section while there are none. The Services page pattern lists its groups this way. New page patterns: Service group (`unhurried/page-service-group`) and Service (`unhurried/page-service`), both built on `unhurried/service-head` (breadcrumbs, the page's own title and excerpt), so no page needs its title retyped. Pages support excerpts (the lede and the card text). The checklist gains an optional "Group your services" step: Add a service group (opens nested under Services) and Add a service. Demo: a flat menu (About, Work, Services, Pricing, Visit, Journal; Topics stays in the footer and the journal toolbar) with one dropdown, Services: Design, Photography, Consulting, two services each. In the editor the section previews recent pages; the live page shows its own. Footer menus (all three footers) are plain links: `showSubmenuIcon: false` and `.unhurried-nav-plain` hides submenus, so only the header has the dropdown.
- **Newsletter**: `unhurried_newsletter_form()` supplies a signup form from a plugin, since a theme may not collect addresses: the free, self-hosted Newsletter plugin's minimal form (a `[newsletter_form type="minimal"]` Shortcode block; `unhurried_render_newsletter_shortcode()` renders it inside template parts, which run shortcodes before their patterns, and names the email field), else Jetpack's Subscribe block, else block markup from the `unhurried_newsletter_block` filter; with none, a "Subscribe by RSS" button. Used by the footer row and the Newsletter band (`unhurried/signup-cta`, which lost its sample mailto button). Styled as the search pill. Newsletter joins the About screen's recommended plugins, and the Playground demo installs it.
- **Motion system.** Tokens in `theme.json` (`settings.custom.motion`: easeOut, easeInOut, durationPress 120ms, durationQuick 220ms, durationReveal 900ms, durationSlow 1400ms, stagger 70ms, distance 28px). `assets/js/reveal.js` (same handle) now marks every element in `main` and the footer with `data-uh` and a kind (rise, card, image, draw, count, words) and reveals it with an IntersectionObserver at 15% visibility; items entering together are staggered, capped at 8 steps; a marked element's descendants stay still; header, navigation, marquee, the spiral disc and an article's body paragraphs never move. `unhurried_motion_head()` prints a tiny inline script that adds `uh-motion` to `<html>` before first paint; filter `unhurried_motion`. (The first version held the first screen back until the script ran, with a 2.5 s failsafe; that changed before release: nothing is held back now, see First paint, F-22, below.) Reduced motion, no script, the editor and print all show everything. The opt-in Reveal style still works and steps aside when the automatic mode is on. Feedback layer: buttons lift on hover and press on click (inside the 400ms Doherty window), link and menu underlines glide, dropdowns unfold, linked cards rise, post photos lean in, chips and fields answer, accordions open with `interpolate-size`, the gift card tilts toward the pointer with a glare, the map pin pings. Scroll-linked depth (`animation-timeline`, where supported): cover photos drift, the staggered gallery floats at two speeds, the hero sinks and its words recede, the header shadow deepens. Cross-document view transitions cross-fade pages while the header and marquee hold still. Opt out per block with `unhurried-still`.
- **Setup checklist** (`inc/setup.php`) on Appearance > About Unhurried, replacing the three-line Start here card: nine steps (name, homepage, posts page, pages, menu, social profiles, newsletter, forms, look) with live status, a progress bar and one button each. Create buttons open `post-new.php?post_type=page&unhurried_page=<slug>`; `unhurried_prefill_page()` (on `default_content`) fills the new auto-draft with the page pattern and sets its template, and `unhurried_prefill_title()` names it. Nothing is created until the user publishes, and a done step drops its Create button. `unhurried_site_editor_url()` builds Site Editor links for both route styles (6.6/6.7 and 6.8+). Filter: `unhurried_setup_steps`. A dismissible dashboard and Themes notice links to it until the steps are done.
- **Contact forms**: `unhurried_contact_form()` puts the oldest Contact Form 7 or WPForms form in the Contact card (`unhurried/contact-split`), else the Email us button; filter `unhurried_contact_form_block`. The checklist recommends Contact Form 7 on PHP 8.3+ (its current requirement) and WPForms Lite below. Plugins are detected by constant, not shortcode, so the Site Editor resolves the same markup as the front end (Newsletter registers its shortcode on the front end only; saving the footer in the editor used to swap the form for the RSS button). Plain form fields and submit buttons from any plugin take the theme's look at zero specificity (`:where()`), with Contact Form 7 label spacing.
- **Anchors**: `scroll-padding-top` clears the sticky header; smooth scrolling unless reduced motion.
- Playground demo: PHP 8.3, Contact Form 7 installed.
- **Link labels**: buttons and links that lead to a page now name it (Contact us, Our services, Read the journal →, Get directions, Email us) in every hero, the call to action, process panel, services accordion, gift card, pricing tiers, story rows, essay, map and contact patterns.
- **Footer**: a Newsletter row under the search (see above). Social icons (Google Business Profile, Instagram, Facebook, X, Pinterest, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Yelp) ship with "#" addresses; `unhurried_hide_placeholder_social()` hides those from visitors (people who can edit the site still see them, and the Playground demo shows all via `UNHURRIED_DEMO`), and an icon block left empty prints nothing.
- **Wordmark**: capitals (`text-transform: uppercase`), and the font size is now exactly `min(100cqi, 1360px) / --unhurried-wordmark-em`, with the em width measured in capitals, so any title fills the column. On hover (or keyboard focus) the letters roll up one after another and an accent-colored twin rises into place; `unhurried_wordmark_letters()` wraps the letters (`aria-hidden`) and repeats the title in a visually hidden span. Reduced motion: an accent color change instead.
- **Hero, statue** (`unhurried/hero-statue`) is the Homepage hero now: a Cover filling an inset, rounded frame at full screen height (less the header, plus the rising panel's overlap), the photo edge to edge, a shade that leans to the lower left under the headline, lede, buttons and a caption. The image is a 19th-century print of Michelangelo's David from the Rijksmuseum (CC0), head only. Phones get a bottom shade instead. Motion: the image unveils once; reduced motion: still.
- **Hero, spiral** (`unhurried/hero-spiral`), a second hero pattern: an inset Ink stage, a round image (`assets/images/spiral.webp`) turning once every 240s inside a hairline and a counter-turning dashed ring, the heading's italic word absolutely placed behind it (heading kept `position: static; z-index: auto`, since flex items honor z-index). `--uh-under` makes room for a following `.unhurried-rise` panel. Reduced motion: still.
- **Journal**: `core/breadcrumbs` on the posts page, archives, search and posts (`prefersTaxonomy` on posts); `unhurried/journal-toolbar` (All chip, `core/terms-query` category chips with counts, posts-only `core/search`, "All topics" link when /topics/ exists); cards gain the category chip and `core/post-time-to-read`; `core/query-total` above the grid; topic chips in the no-results state. `unhurried_mark_current_term()` (`render_block_core/term-name`) marks the current topic with `is-current` and `aria-current`.
- **Posts**: author name, date and reading time; tag chips; `core/post-author` box with bio; `unhurried/hidden-related-posts` (up to three from the same categories, nothing when none); a scroll-linked reading progress bar (`body.single-post::before`, `animation-timeline: scroll(root)`, behind `@supports`).
- **Topics directory** (`unhurried/page-topics`): category cards from `core/terms-query` (description, count via `unhurried_term_count_label()`, translated singular/plural) and tag chips.
- **Essay** (`unhurried/text-essay`): sticky aside, drop-cap lede, two text columns, pull quote, link to the journal. Accordion and gallery sections gain ledes; Services, accordion gains a hairline button.
- **Footer**: tagline plus a pill search, Explore, Topics (`core/categories` with counts) and Latest (`core/latest-posts`, three with dates), shown only when the site has posts; bottom row is the year and site name, Back to top (`#top`) and the credit.
- **Header**: the sticky element is now the template part wrapper (`header.wp-block-template-part:has(> .unhurried-header)`); the capsule was its only child and never stuck. `unhurried_mark_contact_link()` (`render_block_core/navigation`) tags the menu item that links to /contact/ so the capsule hides it beside the Contact button and the open mobile menu keeps it.
- These blocks need WordPress 6.9 or later to show (breadcrumbs, terms query, time to read, query total); on older versions they render nothing.
- Images: a warm still-life and light set (dried stems in vases, sand, leaf shadows, an abstract render for the hero disc), CC0 from StockSnap.io and WordPress.org Photos. Same filenames as 1.1.x, so pages built from older patterns pick up the new pictures.
- **WooCommerce, legibility only** (`inc/woocommerce.php`, new; the shop's layout stays Unhurried Pro's work). In a dark look (Nocturne, Moss, Harbor, Plum, Lagoon, Dusk, or a palette darkened by hand) the Checkout block's floating labels took the page's light text and sat on WooCommerce's white fields at about 1.1:1. `unhurried_is_dark_look()` compares the palette's `base` and `contrast`; when the page is the darker one the theme declares WordPress's own `dark-editor-style` support, which WooCommerce reads as the default of the Cart and Checkout blocks' "Dark mode inputs", so those blocks use WooCommerce's dark fields. `assets/css/woocommerce-dark.css` (dark looks) mixes WooCommerce's red and green halfway to the text color (`--wc-red`: required marks, field errors, the remove cross, 2:1 before; `--wc-green`: the edge of a checked field; both read from WooCommerce's own variables on the root through `--uh-wc-red` and `--uh-wc-green`, so the file holds no color of its own) and gives the white account fields an edge that shows against the page (`--wc-form-border-color`). `assets/css/woocommerce.css` (every look) raises the cart's quantity stepper to 3:1 (its edge was 30% of the text color) and its minus and plus to 4.5:1, and does the same for the older `[woocommerce_cart]` quantity and coupon fields. Both files are printed inline. None of this loads without WooCommerce or under Unhurried Pro; filters `unhurried_is_dark_look` and `unhurried_woocommerce_legibility`.
- **Translated sites**: the checklist found its pages by English slug while their titles, and so their addresses, are translated ("Контакт", /контакт/), so the pages step never completed, the header button never turned on and pattern buttons led to `#`. `unhurried_prefill_page()` now marks each page it opens with its key (post meta `_unhurried_page`), and `unhurried_find_page()` returns the page with the slug, as before, or else the marked one. `unhurried_page_link()`, `unhurried_page_exists()`, the header button and `unhurried_mark_contact_link()` all go through it. Sites with the English slugs behave as before. New filters `unhurried_page_id` (for multilingual plugins) and `unhurried_page_link`.

The review notice names the real wait when `unhurried_notice_days` changes it.

- **First paint (F-22).** The first screen no longer waits for `reveal.js`. Before, `html.uh-motion` held every heading, paragraph, picture and list item in `main` and the footer at opacity 0 until the deferred script ran (or the 2.5 s failsafe fired); a browser does not count an element at opacity 0 as painted, so on a slow phone the page's largest paint waited for the script. Now the first screen rises by CSS alone from the first paint: the outermost block of each kind runs `uh-arrive` (text), `uh-arrive-far` (pictures) or `uh-arrive-card` (cards, panels, details), from opacity .4 and 28 or 42 px below its place over `--uh-reveal` (900 ms), as DESIGN-GUIDE section 6 asks for the first screen. `reveal.js` then marks (`data-uh`) only what is below the window when it runs; what is on screen or above it finishes the CSS rise. The failsafe timer is gone: nothing is hidden before the script runs. With JavaScript off, the script blocked, reduced motion or in print everything shows, as before. Dividers and the wordmark still draw in from the script; a figure on the first screen rises with it and shows its final value from the first paint instead of counting up (a decision, F-33: the first screen must read at once, DESIGN-GUIDE section 6, and counting from the first paint would turn the figure already on screen back to 0 when the script arrives); figures below the window count up as before and end as written. The journal's results count (`.wp-block-query-total`) is in the CSS list too, so it rises with the grid instead of standing still beside it (f22 judge, p3). Lighthouse, phone, free alone, median of 5: home LCP 6013 to 4519 ms, About 5566 to 3026 ms, a post 3629 to 3339 ms; FCP unchanged.

The first picture keeps `fetchpriority="high"`. Both run as the page is shown, so pages built from older patterns benefit too; Media Library images keep WordPress's own rules.

Over the f33 judge's three photographs, small text measures 3.03 to 9.64:1: 7.73 to 9.64 on the pale one at 50%, 5.31 to 6.99 on the mid-tone one at 70% and 4.05 to 5.27 at 50%, 3.03 to 4.49 on the dark one at 50% (F-48 re-measure); where it stays under 4.5:1, light text measured lower still, 1.2 to 1.8:1 (f33 judge). A Cover set to a light shade (`is-light`) and blocks with a fill of their own inside a Cover keep their colors. Light looks load nothing new.
