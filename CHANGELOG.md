# Changelog

All notable changes to the free Unhurried theme.

## 1.2.1 (unreleased)

- **Theme URI is https://unhurried.skin** (was https://switchcasestudio.com/projects/unhurried), the theme's own site (owner's decision, 2026-11-26). Author URI is unchanged.
- **Accessibility-ready groundwork** (WordPress.org's 18 requirements; the `accessibility-ready` tag itself is not added, as the handbook asks, until the accessibility team has reviewed the theme):
  - `accessibility.txt` (new), in the theme's root in the wpaccessibility.org format: how the theme was tested, what was not done (no screen reader pass, no testing with assistive technology users, no outside audit), its features, the screen reader text class, and where to ask for help or report a problem (hello@switchcasestudio.com).
  - `.screen-reader-text` and its `:focus` rule are in `style.css`, with the properties the requirement names (the focus box in the look's Base and Contrast); WordPress's own copy is no longer the only one. `.unhurried-visually-hidden` stays.
  - `add_theme_support( 'html5', ... )` in `unhurried_setup()` lists `navigation-widgets` with WordPress's own list for block themes (WordPress merges the two, so nothing is dropped): a classic Navigation Menu widget prints a `<nav>`.
  - **No sideways scroll after the window narrows.** The two columns of a split layout that had not yet entered kept the sideways offset chosen at load (`reveal.js`: 782px or wider, 64px of room), so narrowing an open page from 1280 to 640 or 320 without a reload (as 200% and 400% zoom do) scrolled it sideways by 16 to 31px until they entered. `reveal.js` now checks again when it marks them and `reveal-late.js` on every resize; a column without the room rises instead, its offset gone at once and its row clipped sideways for that one layout (WebKit otherwise kept the page as wide as the old offset had made it). Measured on 10 pages narrowed right after load, at 640 and 320, with and without the WCAG text spacing settings: 16 to 31px before, 0 after, in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit.
  - **Links in text keep their underline under the pointer**, a step heavier (`max(2px, .08em)`), instead of losing it, so a hovered link still differs from its words by more than color. `theme.json` no longer sets the hover's `textDecoration` to none; a link without an underline (menus, buttons, titles, chips, the footer's lists) keeps its own hover.
  - **The Unhurried Pro card and notice open the Pro page in the same tab.** They opened a new tab, with the warning for screen readers only.
  - **Reduced motion asked for while a page is open** shows at once what still waited below the window (it showed only once it was reached); `reveal-late.js` listens for the change. With reduced motion from the start nothing ever waited.
  - **A block marked `unhurried-still` holds still, with everything in it.** Only the entrances honored the class: inside such a block a Cover's photograph kept drifting with the scroll, and so did framed photos (a staggered gallery's too), a step's bar and the spiral hero's disc and light; the statue hero's photograph still settled as the page loaded, an eyebrow that carries the class still drew its rule, and a moving line kept moving. One rule in `style.css` now stops every CSS animation on such a block, inside it and on their `::before` and `::after`. Not on `body`, where purchase pages carry the class for their entrances only, so every block without the class moves as before. What answers the pointer or the keyboard (a photo easing closer, the wordmark's roll) still moves, and a film plays until it is paused; `accessibility.txt` and About Unhurried now say exactly that.
  - **An accordion answer never stays invisible.** `reveal.js` no longer plans an entrance for what a closed `<details>` holds (the question in its `<summary>` still enters): such an answer shows when opened, with the opening fade from `scroll.css`. An answer planned while closed (an FAQ item across the bottom of the first screen) waited for an intersection report that Chromium did not always send after the item was opened, and stayed at opacity 0 (the release candidate review, 4 of 4 runs).
- **About Unhurried credits the photographs to their sources**, the Cleveland Museum of Art's Open Access collection and the Rijksmuseum; it still named StockSnap.io and the WordPress.org Photo Directory from before 1.2.0's photo series. The repository's README says WordPress 7.0 or newer and 58 patterns.
- **The Unhurried Pro card no longer promises a 14-day money-back guarantee.** All three of its wordings (`shop`, `time`, `proof` in `unhurried_pro_card()`) say one payment and all sales are final, as the sales page does (owner's decision, 2026-11-26). The `time` wording had kept "14 days to change your mind". The notice shown with WooCommerce names no terms.
- **Policy page, and Privacy, Terms and Returns in the footer.** A new page pattern, Policy page (`unhurried/page-policy`), lays out a privacy, terms or returns page under the default title head: the date it was last updated, a short introduction, a contents list that links to six numbered sections (beside the text and in view from 782px, above it on a phone) and a closing line that links to Contact, all in Lorem ipsum for the owner's own words. The three footers list Privacy, Terms and Returns beside their bottom line's first item once those pages are published (the page in Settings > Privacy or at /privacy/, and the pages at /terms/ and /returns/; `inc/legal.php`, new, with the `unhurried_legal_links` filter); a site without them keeps its footer exactly as it looked, and a footer saved in the Site Editor is not touched.
- **The header fits a phone at large page zoom.** Narrower than 320px (Safari's page zoom at 200% gives a 195px window) the site title shows whole, the header button that goes to Contact steps aside when the menu holds a Contact link, and what still does not fit moves to a second row of the capsule: nothing runs past the screen. From 320px the header is unchanged.
- **Pages fit that window too:** under 320px a word or a link wider than its column breaks and the topics grid gives way to the window, and on a phone the journal's search is never wider than its line (Safari scrolled the journal sideways by 6px at 320px), so no page scrolls sideways.
- **The moving line starts again at once after Play on a touch screen.** It held still until the reader tapped elsewhere; a hovering pointer and the keyboard still hold it.
- **A tapped control no longer looks pressed on a touch screen:** Hairline buttons, menu links, the footer's social icons, form buttons and post-term chips keep their resting look after a tap (their hover now needs a pointer that hovers).
- **The homepage film never plays on a phone held sideways.** It needs a window at least 480px tall as well; tablets in landscape and desktops keep it.
- **The homepage hero fits a phone held sideways.** Under 480px tall the words start near the top of the stage and sit close, so the title, the lede and the buttons show on the first screen (at 750 × 330 whole, and their labels even with Safari's landscape tab bar at 280px tall).
- **The homepage hero reads in every direction and on every phone.** Right to left, the stage's shade and the film's follow the words to the right, with a little more in the caption's corner (the headline fell to 1.5:1 over the face); on a phone held upright the shade holds behind the eyebrow; the header steps aside again after the phone menu is closed by a tap; and the film's stylesheet no longer holds up a phone's first paint.
- **Larger touch targets.** On a touch screen the menu's open and close buttons, the header button, an accordion's question, topic chips and the film's pause button take taps across 44px, with nothing moving or looking different, except that rows of topic chips stand 3px further apart there. Beside a long or cut site title the menu button leaves the title its taps (40px wide at the least).
- **A live map can go full screen** (its frame now allows it, at whatever address Google moves the map to; Safari logged a refused permission).
- **The wordmark keeps its own pace and color.** The footer wordmark and the header title roll at their 1.2.0 timing again (0.9s, letters 35ms apart; the entrance 1.2s, 45ms apart) and the footer twin is the accent itself. The motion pass had shortened the roll to 320ms and mixed the twin halfway toward the text color. A wordmark is a logotype, so the contrast minimum for text does not apply to it.
- **The homepage's hero can play a film behind its words.** The Homepage pattern (`unhurried/page-home`) opens on Hero, statue (`unhurried/hero-statue`) as in 1.2.0: Michelangelo's David filling the rounded, inset stage, the words at the lower left, the caption at the lower right. Its Cover now holds a Video block named "Film (optional)", first among its words: empty as shipped (the theme ships no film), it shows nothing on the page (no player, no button, no request: the 1.2.0 hero as it was), and in the editor it is a small "Video" label above the eyebrow that opens the Video block's upload and library buttons. A site owner sets, swaps or removes the film there in the Site Editor (a landscape loop, 16:9, black and white suits the photographs). On the page `unhurried_hero_film_stage()` (`render_block_core/cover`) moves the film out of the words to cover the whole stage, over the photograph and under the shade and the words; the photograph shows first and stays without scripts, with reduced motion, with data saving and until the film plays, and the film plays (after the page has loaded, while in view, muted, looping, inline) only where the stage suits a landscape film: 782px and wider, in a window wider than tall. On a phone or an upright tablet it would be cropped to under half its width and lose the face as it turns, so the photograph stays there and the film is never loaded. The film brings a shade of its own that fades in and out with it, darkest under the words and lighter where the face turns, so every word and both buttons stay at WCAG AA over its brightest frames in all 20 looks; the caption is .85 opaque with a film (.7 on the photograph). The pause and play button sits beside the caption, never on the words, in a place kept for it. Phones held upright now load the part of the photograph they show, cut at 1:2 from the same master, through a picture element (`unhurried_theme_image_srcset()`, for any Cover picture with a `-portrait` copy beside it): `assets/images/david-mono-portrait-500.webp` (500x1000, 26,544 bytes) up to 1.75x and `david-mono-portrait.webp` (960x1920, 70,698 bytes) on denser screens, where they loaded the 1200 copy (71,648 bytes) or the whole 1800 picture before; tablets and wider load what they did. The statue hero's photo rules reach a picture-wrapped image too.
- **Hero, framed picture** (`unhurried/hero-film`, new; a pattern to choose, the homepage keeps Hero, statue): Michelangelo's David in a rounded 4:5 frame (the card radius) beside an eyebrow, a three-line headline, a lede and one button, with a caption under the frame ("Michelangelo, *David*, Florence. Photograph: Edizione Brogi, Rijksmuseum"). Never edge to edge, never under the words, and never taller than the window leaves room for; side by side down to 600px, then the words and a square frame (at most 30rem) under them on a phone. The picture is new crops of the theme's own David photograph (the Rijksmuseum print `david-mono.webp` is cut from, CC0), made the way the 1.2.0 set was: `assets/images/david-hero.webp` (1024x1280, 69,760 bytes) and `david-hero-800.webp` (800x1000, 45,410 bytes) for the 4:5 frame, and `david-hero-square.webp` (800x800, 39,974 bytes) and `david-hero-square-660.webp` (660x660, 26,384 bytes, for phones up to about 1.75x) for the square phone frame: no heavier than the 1.2.0 hero's copies for the same screens, and the phone copy no heavier than the picture those phones loaded before. The face sits right of center, looking into the frame, with the curls whole.
- **The framed picture can play a film the site owner adds, too.** The frame is a Group of an Image block (the picture) and a Video block (named "Film (optional)" in the List View), so both are swapped in the editor. The theme ships no film: the Video block is empty, and an empty one shows nothing on the page (the picture alone: no player, no button, no request, the same layout), while the editor shows its "upload or choose a video" box at the foot of the picture. To add a film: select it, then Upload or Media Library (a short silent loop, 4:5, black and white suits the photos). With a film, the picture still shows first and stays without scripts, with reduced motion, with data saving (`navigator.connection.saveData`, `prefers-reduced-data`) and until the film plays. `inc/hero-film.php` (`unhurried_hero_film_frame()`, on `render_block_core/group`) leaves an empty Video block out, makes a video a silent loop that loads nothing by itself (no autoplay, controls or poster, `preload="none"`, muted, looping, `playsinline`, hidden from screen readers), gives the picture its real width and height, its 800 copy as a srcset and, on phones, the square crop and its 660 copy (a `picture` element, where a `-square` copy sits beside a theme picture), plus `fetchpriority="high"` when the hero is in a template rather than content; it adds the pause and play button after a frame with a film and loads `assets/css/hero-film.css` (inline) with the frame and `assets/js/hero-film.js` (deferred) only with a film. The script starts the film after the page's load event, only while the frame is a quarter in view, pauses it when it leaves, and keeps a visitor's pause (localStorage). The button is a 40px hairline circle like the footer's icons, labeled "Pause the film" or "Play the film"; the caption's row is the button's height with a film or without one, so nothing moves. The headline's lines are short enough to keep their breaks when the fonts arrive (it wrapped differently while Fraunces loaded).
- **Menu dropdowns open when the pointer rests on them.** In the header capsule's inline menu a dropdown and its arrow wait `--uh-intent-in` (100ms, new) before opening and `--uh-intent-out` (250ms, new) before closing, so a pointer crossing the menu opens nothing and one that slips off a dropdown has time to come back. Pointing at the next dropdown swaps the two in one frame. The keyboard waits for neither. With reduced motion the waits stay and nothing moves. CSS on core's own show and hide; core still sets `aria-expanded` the moment the pointer arrives and leaves.
- **Menu items with an arrow look centered in their pill.** The light arrow left the label looking pushed to the left: the pill now has `--uh-pill-lead` (new, `.25rem`) more room before the label and `--uh-pill-trail` (new, `.125rem`) less after the arrow; the margins give it back, so nothing moves at rest. Measured inside the hovered pill at 1440 and 1024: label to the left edge 12 to 12.5px before, 16 to 16.5px now; arrow to the right edge 12.5 to 13px before, 10.5 to 11px now; the gaps between the items at rest unchanged (24.5 to 25.5px). Logical properties, so right to left works.
- **A live Google map, free, in the theme's look.** The Map and directions pattern (`unhurried/map-directions`, name unchanged) shows a live Google map wherever the free Gmap Block plugin (`gmap-block` on WordPress.org: Google Maps without an API key) is active, with the address under it as text (`unhurried-map-caption`, new); without the plugin, the drawn map as before. Its demo place is a point in a park (41.5087, -81.6129), not an address: the old demo address matched a real business, whose card Google showed. Get directions goes to the same point. Patterns inserted earlier keep what they had. `inc/map.php` (new) shows any block that is just a Google map in the theme's frame (`unhurried_map_frame()` on `render_block`; `unhurried-map-frame`, new): the plugin's block, Google Maps' own "Share, Embed a map" code in a Custom HTML block, another plugin's map block. The frame is 4:3 with the card radius, holds its space from the start (no layout shift), and shows the map black and white (`--uh-map-filter`, new), dark with light labels in the six dark looks. The map gets `loading="lazy"`, and the title "Map" where it has none or only coordinates. A block with more than the map in it is left alone. The theme still loads no map service itself. `assets/css/map.css` (the pin), `assets/css/map-live.css` (the frame) and `assets/js/map.js` (new, deferred) load only on pages that show a map: a live map's styles in the head, printed inline, so its frame keeps its shape from the first paint; the drawn map's at the end of the page, so they hold up nothing above it. The readme's new Live Google map section has the steps.
- **The map pin drops in.** A terracotta teardrop pin falls onto the drawn map's place once the map is 40% in view, lands with a small squash, and a ring spreads once on the ground (the ring pinged three times, mostly before anyone scrolled down to it). On a live map of one place it lands on Google's own pin once the map is 40% in view (`unhurried_map_centered()` tells a map of one place from a map of an area, a route or Street View, which keep Google's pins). A live map then waits for the visitor, so it cannot slide from under the pin: the page scrolls over it until a click on the map, the "Pan and zoom" button (new) or keyboard focus moving into it hands it over, and the theme's pin fades to Google's. Keyboard focus inside the map draws the theme's ring around the frame (Google's own ring turns gray with the map). Reduced motion: the pin is simply there. No script: Google's map and pin, usable as they come. Durations from the motion tokens (`--uh-dur-slow`, `--uh-ease-in`, `--uh-ease-out`).
- **Homepage: a map with directions** (`unhurried/map-directions`) between the journal and the closing call to action, the visit moment before "Contact us".
- **Scroll choreography: the page answers as it is scrolled.** A new stylesheet, `assets/css/scroll.css`, printed at the end of the page (`unhurried_scroll_styles()`), so it never holds up the first screen, and new kinds in `assets/js/reveal.js`. Assigned by role: text rises 24px over 560ms; headings arrive line by line, 90ms apart (the words are wrapped in place and read the same; undone if the heading's size would change); photos and covers rise 48px over 760ms while the picture settles from 114%; the two columns of a split layout arrive from their own sides; cards come from 48px and 96%; step numerals tick in; figures count for 1100ms; separators draw across in 900ms. Scroll-linked, where the browser has scroll-driven animations: photos drift 8% inside a frame of their own shape (`unhurried_image_frame()` gives an Image block without a caption, a set size or a border the class `unhurried-frame` and its radius as `--uh-frame-r`); a step's accent bar fills while it crosses the middle of the window; a section's eyebrow gains an accent rule as the section reaches the middle; the spiral hero's disc turns with the scroll instead of for good. New tokens, none changed: `--uh-dur-text`, `--uh-dur-large`, `--uh-dur-draw`, `--uh-dur-count`, `--uh-dur-morph`, `--uh-line-stagger`, `--uh-dist-text`, `--uh-dist-large`, `--uh-settle`, `--uh-zoom-photo`, `--uh-drift`, `--uh-drift-scale`.
- **Motion fixes after the second review.** The film hero's picture no longer drifts, scales or takes its own corner (`unhurried_image_frame()` skips Image blocks whose class names a hero), so the picture and the film's first frame line up and the hand-over shows no ghost. A picture settles from 114% only inside a frame that clips it (a framed photo, a cover), so no page scrolls sideways while photos arrive. The marquee runs in a clipped window of its own (`unhurried-marquee__window`) with the pause button beside it: 44 by 34px in the band's colors, a 44px touch target, a focus ring inside it, and focus can no longer scroll the line. Escape closes an open dropdown from anywhere, also one opened by a resting pointer, and leaves focus where it is. Going from a post or product to another one names only the clicked card's photo and title for the transition (the page's own head lets go). The state fades (an accordion opened, a tab chosen, an option picked, a cart row arriving) play only on a change the reader made, never on what is already on screen when the late stylesheet arrives. Text arrives in 420ms (`--uh-dur-text`, was 560ms) and starts a little before it is in view; cards and split columns keep 560ms (`--uh-dur-card`, new). Whatever a reader scrolled past before the page finished loading is never hidden again. The eyebrow accent rule skips the first screen and draws from the right in right-to-left pages; under the pointer it reaches a little further. Head and cover photographs and an arch picture ease closer under the pointer. The header's hide class sits on the header, not on `<html>` (a root class restyled the whole page). The film's pause circle has a 3:1 edge in every style variation, and its button points at the film with `data-film` (the film is hidden from assistive technology). Menu hover-intent, press, button-hover, gift card and map rules moved from `style.css` to `scroll.css`, so the render-blocking stylesheet is back below its size before the scroll choreography.
- **Motion fixes after the third review.** The entrances are set up in short slices after load, each reading every box it needs before writing, so a phone with a slow processor never waits on them (one 350 to 410ms task on the home page with the CPU 4x slower is gone; with the CPU 4x slower no task of the setup passes 50ms). A heading is wrapped into lines only shortly before it enters, when the page is idle. Everything that answers the reader after load moved to a new file, `assets/js/reveal-late.js`, fetched when the page is idle (it loads `scroll.css`): state fades, the marquee's pause button, Escape for dropdowns, the header that steps aside, the gift card's tilt, headings line by line and figures counting up. `reveal.js` is smaller than in 1.2.0. Photos drift half as far (`--uh-drift` 2%, `--uh-drift-scale` 1.05; covers 107% and 3%). The header returns only after about 120px of reading back up, not on any small nudge. Without JavaScript the marquee stands still (it has no pause button there). A live map gets its address only once it is within about a screen of the window (map.js; a `<noscript>` copy loads it lazily without JavaScript), so the page contacts Google only when the reader nears the map. A row arriving in the mini-cart fades in when the drawer shows it. On phones up to 480px wide the drawn map is square, so its note stays clear of the pin. A section's own photograph, as in the closing call to action, gains a little depth under the pointer and eases closer inside its corners where the browser can (`object-view-box`). The old three-ping ring left in `scroll.css` is gone. The tokens for the larger moves (`--uh-dur-text`, `--uh-dur-card`, `--uh-dur-large`, `--uh-dur-draw`, `--uh-dur-count`, `--uh-dur-morph`, `--uh-line-stagger`, `--uh-dist-text`, `--uh-dist-large`, `--uh-settle`, `--uh-zoom-photo`; values unchanged) moved from `scroll.css` to `style.css`: set on the root by a late stylesheet, they restyled the whole page when it arrived.
- **The header steps aside while reading down** (past 320px) and returns on the way up; it never hides while it holds keyboard focus or an open menu, and moves only by a translate of the sticky header (no layout shift).
- **Pages connect.** A clicked journal card's photo and title become the post's head, and a product card's photo becomes the product gallery's first picture (cross-document view transitions with shared names, 480ms); elsewhere the f66 cross-fade; none into a page held still.
- **Every component answers the pointer.** A framed photo eases forward to 106% inside its frame and its caption darkens; a post card's photo too; a step's numeral takes the accent; table and hours rows light up; search pills, comment fields and Glass accordions answer; a field left wrong shows the accent on its edge. Nothing that leads nowhere gets a pointer cursor or a lift; all of it waits for a pointer.
- **Marquee pause button** (`unhurried-marquee__pause`, from `unhurried_marquee_loop()`): the moving line can be stopped (WCAG 2.2.2).
- **Product pages move below the buy area.** `unhurried_woocommerce_is_purchase_page()` no longer counts a product: the first screen and the columns holding its form stay still, the description, tabs and related products move; jQuery's animations stay off there.
- **The first screen arrives in order** on every page: headings and eyebrows at once (an LCP candidate is never held back), then text (+120ms), pictures and post cards (+160ms), controls (+220ms), through `--uh-arrive-delay`; the header capsule drops in when someone enters the site, not between its pages. State changes fade in: a variable product's price, a cart row, an opened accordion's answer, a shown product tab. The entrances are set up after the page has loaded, and `assets/css/scroll.css` loads then too (`unhurried_scroll_styles()` hands its address to the script; a `noscript` link keeps the pointer responses without JavaScript).
- Reduced motion, no script and print: everything shows, nothing moves. Arriving at an `#anchor` or going Back: nothing replays.
- **Motion tokens: one calm set for everything that moves.** New custom properties on `:root` (style.css, Motion system): `--uh-dur-press` 100ms (a press), `--uh-dur-quick` 160ms (color, underlines, borders), `--uh-dur-base` 240ms (a control changing state), `--uh-dur-panel` 320ms (a surface arriving), `--uh-dur-slow` 420ms (entrances, a photo settling), `--uh-exit` .65 (an exit takes 65% of its entrance: `calc(var(--uh-dur-base) * var(--uh-exit))`), `--uh-stagger` 40ms and `--uh-stagger-cap` 160ms, `--uh-ease-out` `cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)`, `--uh-ease-in` `cubic-bezier(.4,0,1,1)`, `--uh-ease-in-out` `cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1)`, `--uh-dist-reveal` 16px, `--uh-dist-panel` 8px, `--uh-press-y` 1px, `--uh-press-scale` .98, `--uh-zoom` 1.03, `--uh-lift` -3px. Nothing is renamed: the 1.2.0 names (`--uh-ease`, `--uh-ease-io`, `--uh-press`, `--uh-quick`, `--uh-reveal`, `--uh-slow`, `--uh-rise`) now follow the new ones, and `theme.json`'s `settings.custom.motion` values point at them (new keys `easeIn`, `durationBase`, `durationPanel`, `exit`), so `--wp--custom--motion--*` gives the same numbers. Every transition and animation in the theme uses them; measured on 21 page types at 1440 and 8 at 390: no transition off the tokens.
- **Calmer, shorter motion.** Entrances take 420ms (were 900ms, dividers and photos 1.4s, the wordmark's letters 1.2s), rise 16px (was 28px), and items entering together start 40ms apart, never more than 160ms after the first (were 70ms, up to 560ms), so a batch settles within 580ms; figures count up in the same 580ms (were 1.4s). Buttons change color under the pointer and no longer lift; a press sinks them 1px to 98% within 100ms and they spring back on release (submit buttons, the search button, the menu's open and close buttons and the footer's social icons too). Dropdowns rise 8px into place in 240ms and leave in 156ms. Accordions open in 320ms and close in 208ms. A linked card rises 3px (was 6px), a photo leans in to 103% (was 104%). The wordmark's and the header title's roll take 320ms with their letters 20ms apart (at most 160ms), and roll back in 208ms (was 900ms with no cap). The phone menu fades and rises in over 320ms (core's 100ms). Notices (WooCommerce's, Contact Form 7's) fade in from .4. The map pin rings three times, then rests (it looped for good). The topic cards' lift now waits for a pointer and respects reduced motion.
- **Heroes paint whole.** The statue hero's photograph and the spiral hero's disc are never dimmed, darkened or blurred on arrival (they came up from opacity .4 and 15 to 30% brightness over 2.8 to 3.2s): they only settle from 104% in 420ms, and the disc starts turning at once. The heroes' accent word no longer "exhales" (it animated letter-spacing and a blur for 3.4s, which reflowed the headline); the `unhurried-exhale` keyframes stay for anything that names them.
- **Page cross-fade, shorter and still.** The old page fades out in 156ms while the new one fades in over 240ms, with no slide (was 320ms and 550ms with a 12px and 18px slide). A page whose body has the class `unhurried-still` (WooCommerce's purchase pages, and Unhurried Pro's shop) arrives without a cross-fade.
- **Purchase pages hold still without Pro.** With WooCommerce active, a product, the cart, the checkout and the account page get the body class `unhurried-still` and no scroll-in entrances (`unhurried_woocommerce_is_purchase_page()`, on the `unhurried_motion` and `body_class` filters). Controls there still answer the pointer and the keyboard.
- **Reduced motion means no movement at all.** Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the duration tokens drop to 100ms or less, the distances to 0, and one rule keeps only color, background, border, outline, underline, fill, opacity and shadow changes in any transition, the theme's or a block's or plugin's, in 100ms (`!important`, so the visitor's setting wins). The reading progress bar on posts is not drawn (the browser's scroll bar says the same). Measured on 21 page types: 0 elements with a moving transition or running moving animation.
- **Motion fixes after review.** Header menu pill: the fill fades but the label switches color in one step at the moment that keeps it at 3.26:1 or more in every frame of every light look and 3.33:1 in the dark ones (new `--uh-pill-switch-in` and `--uh-pill-switch-out`, 39 and 9ms; 22 and 20ms in a dark look through `assets/css/dark-covers.css`); before, label and fill cross-faded through the same lightness (1.07:1 at worst). Hover fills (menu pill, topic cards) now need a pointer, so a tap no longer leaves them filled.
